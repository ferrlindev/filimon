<objective>
Harden component loading to the HOST-01 contract: only what the registry lists and enables is opened; source and fetcher components are validated against their WIT world without running guest code; WASI and any foreign import are rejected with a clear, named error.

Purpose: success criterion 1 ("listed and enabled components are loaded; a `.wasm` that sits next to them but is not in the registry is never instantiated") plus the WASI policy that was an open blocker in STATE.md.
Output: preflight and fetcher loading in `loader.rs`, the `fetcher` world and its linker in `engine.rs`, two error variants in `error.rs`, and tests. Most tests need no prebuilt wasm: garbage files prove the opened-path boundary, and a tiny component compiled from one line of WAT proves the WASI rejection.

Decisions recorded (RESEARCH Discretion Resolutions 1 and 3, A7):
- Reject WASI rather than link a locked-down WASI context: smallest attack surface, matches PROJECT.md "every capability is an explicit WIT import". The direction is reversible (allowing a locked-down WASI later is additive; rejecting later would break plugins).
- "Loading a fetcher" before Phase 5 means: open the file, preflight the imports, instantiate against the `fetcher` world, keep the `FetcherPre`. No routing.
- A fetcher that fails to load is reported by name and logged; it does not stop sources.
</objective>
