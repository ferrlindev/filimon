<objective>
Close the one remaining verification gap of Phase 2 (review WR-12). Two parts of src/commands/consume.rs interact badly. `decode_social` emits a social record's notes (claims-news, published-set-to-null) as separate stream items ahead of the record. `collect_records` charges each note to the diagnostic budget and checks the budget and the limit between items. So a stop can land between a note and its record. When that happens, the record is not written, yet its note (kept, or named in a "noted" range) tells the operator the record was written with published null. The verifier reproduced this at the budget boundary: records=1 for an in-range published and records=0 for an out-of-range one. That breaks 02-06 prohibition #2 and 02-05 truth #5. In the merged run there is a second way in: `merge_incoming` polls news and social in turn, so a news record that fills the limit, or a news skip that hits the budget, can also land between a social note and its record.

Purpose: after this plan, a social record and its notes are one stream item. No stop can separate them, and every note names a record that is in the output. Notes stop counting toward the diagnostic budget, since they are bounded by the records. The work limit on undecodable values, the message cap, the offset ranges, content-freedom, the single stderr choke point, streaming output and the 02-06 year guard stay as they are.
Output: changes to `src/commands/consume.rs` only: one `collect_records` arm (Task 1); the `Incoming::Noted` variant replacing the standalone note variant, `decode_social` returning one item, `social_incoming`, `collect_records` and `consume_stream` taking that item whole (Task 2); three new tests and four updated tests.
</objective>
