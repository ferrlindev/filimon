<objective>
Close verification gap 3 of Phase 2 (review WR-09): D-09 promises `published` is "RFC 3339 or null", but chrono writes a year outside 0000..=9999 with a sign and five or more digits. Valid RFC 3339 input near the year limits therefore leaves the pipeline as text no RFC 3339 parser accepts: on the social side through `rfc3339_to_utc` (since WR-04) and `epoch_ms_to_rfc3339`, and on the news side through `parse_news_published` (since ce9a969).

Purpose: after this plan, all three conversions format through one guarded helper and give None when the UTC year is outside 0..=9999. On the social decode path that None takes the existing route: `published` null plus one diagnostic naming the topic and offset. On the news path it is null with the original string kept in `article.published_raw` (D-09). In-range limits keep converting, and WR-04's UTC normalization stays (gap 1 override).
Output: changes to `src/models.rs` (one private helper, three call sites, extended and new tests) and `src/commands/consume.rs` (the published note's wording and one test).
</objective>
