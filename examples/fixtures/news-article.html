Parliament passes national climate policy bill

The government won a decisive vote in parliament today on a national climate policy bill that sets new targets for energy production and emissions. Ministers said the policy would strengthen the economy and protect the environment ahead of next year's election.

A research report published alongside the bill found that investment in clean energy could add thousands of jobs. Opposition members questioned the cost to the economy, but the government said the climate research supported its plan.

The bill now goes to the upper house, where a final vote is expected next month.
